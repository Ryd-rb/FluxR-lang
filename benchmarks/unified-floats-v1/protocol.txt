FluxR 1 accepts finite decimal binary32 literals ending in f: optional minus, decimal digits, optional dot and digits, optional e/E exponent with optional sign and digits. A decimal literal has at most 256 bytes including its suffix, admitted before numeric conversion. Decimal conversion rounds directly to binary32, preserving negative zero; overflow is rejected and underflow rounds normally. Exact 0xHEXf literals remain available for every bit pattern including infinities and NaN payloads. Decimal spellings produce the same single F32 AST node as the corresponding bit literal. Canonical current-source printing selects a shorter decimal only after exact bit roundtrip; FA1 and WK1 retain their readers and raw-bit printing. Counts, type checks, node/depth bounds, execution charges, arithmetic traps and canonical artifacts are unchanged.
